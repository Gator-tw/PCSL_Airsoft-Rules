# PCSL Airsoft Rulebook - Markdown (2026)

This repository contains the 2026 PCSL (Practical Competition Shooting League) General Rulebook converted into a structured Markdown format, together with community-developed Airsoft-specific rule and division modifications.

The project is designed to provide a clean, structured, and machine-readable version of the PCSL rules for human reference, GitBook publishing, AI-assisted retrieval, and community discussion.

## Objective

The primary purpose of this repository is to maintain a high-quality Markdown version of the PCSL rulebook that maximizes:

- Human readability
- Cross-reference accuracy
- Markdown compatibility
- GitBook publishing compatibility
- AI / LLM parsing reliability
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) performance
- Long-term version control and community review

The PCSL General Rulebook remains the foundation of this project.

Airsoft-specific rules are implemented through dedicated appendices rather than rewriting the General Rulebook itself.

## Repository Structure

### PCSL General Rules

The following files reproduce the PCSL General Rulebook:

- Chapters 1–17
- Appendix A — Pistol Divisions
- Appendix B — Rifle Divisions
- Appendix C — Shotgun Divisions
- Appendix D — 3Gun-Specific Division Modifications
- Appendix E — Changelog

These sections are intended to remain aligned as closely as possible with the current official PCSL General Rulebook.

### PCSL Airsoft Modifications

Two additional appendices provide the Airsoft-specific rules used by this project:

- **Appendix F — PCSL Airsoft Modifications**
- **Appendix G — PCSL Airsoft Division Modifications**

These appendices adapt the General Rulebook to the mechanical, safety, equipment, and scoring characteristics of Airsoft platforms.

## How the Airsoft Appendices Are Applied

The General Rulebook remains applicable unless a rule is specifically modified, replaced, or superseded by an Airsoft appendix.

The rules should therefore be read in the following order:

1. **PCSL General Rulebook**
2. **Appendix F — PCSL Airsoft Modifications**
3. **Appendix G — PCSL Airsoft Division Modifications**

### Appendix F

Appendix F modifies general rules that require different treatment for Airsoft platforms.

Examples include:

- Loaded, unloaded, and inert firearm definitions
- Drop-Mag Target (DMT) procedures
- Eye and ear protection requirements
- Minimum steel engagement distances
- Airsoft chronograph and kinetic-energy limits
- Airsoft-specific stage procedures
- Airsoft target and scoring adaptations

Where Appendix F explicitly modifies or supersedes a General Rulebook provision, **Appendix F takes precedence for Airsoft matches**.

### Appendix G

Appendix G modifies equipment and division requirements for Airsoft platforms.

It applies primarily to the divisions defined in:

- Appendix A — Pistol Divisions
- Appendix B — Rifle Divisions

Examples include:

- Airsoft handgun equipment requirements
- Slide and barrel configuration rules
- PCC classification
- Airsoft rifle equipment restrictions
- Magazine capacity
- Compensators and muzzle devices
- Airsoft-specific division equipment criteria

Where Appendix G explicitly modifies or supersedes a division rule in Appendix A or Appendix B, **Appendix G takes precedence for Airsoft matches**.

## Rule Precedence

For Airsoft use, rule precedence is:

**Airsoft-specific rule > corresponding General Rulebook rule**

This does not mean that Appendix F or Appendix G replaces the entire General Rulebook.

Only provisions specifically addressed by the Airsoft appendices are modified.

All other General Rulebook provisions remain in effect.

For example:

> If Appendix F modifies Section 1.8, Appendix F governs that subject for Airsoft matches.

However:

> If Appendix F contains no modification to another provision in Chapter 1, the original General Rulebook provision continues to apply.

The same principle applies to Appendix G and the standard PCSL division rules.

- Markdown heading structure
