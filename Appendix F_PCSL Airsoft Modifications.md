# Appendix F: PCSL Airsoft

To accommodate the unique mechanical realities of Airsoft platforms, this appendix establishes specific annotations that modify or supersede the PCSL General Rulebook where applicable, aligning them with practical Airsoft officiating procedures.

## F.1 Range Safety & Procedures Modifications

The following rules modify or supersede their respective sections in [Chapter 1](01_Range%20Safety%20&%20Procedures.md) for all affiliated Airsoft matches.

### F.1.1 Loaded firearms

Section [1.4.1.2](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.4.1.2-brass-casing-present>) does not apply to Airsoft matches. The condition of a loaded firearm is determined solely by [Section 1.4.1.1](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.4.1.1-chambered-cartridge>) (a projectile is chambered or otherwise inside the magwell or action) and [Section 1.4.1.3](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.4.1.3-magazine-inserted>).

### F.1.2 Inert Firearms and DMT Process

Modifying [Section 1.4.2.2](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.4.2.2-the-dmt-process-is-used-rifle-only>): Due to Airsoft platforms utilizing propellant housed entirely within the magazine, a firearm is deemed an **Inert Firearm** immediately upon the complete removal of its magazine, regardless of whether a chambered projectile remains. The competitor may then abandon or transition the firearm according to the stage procedure.

*(Note: If a formal Drop-Mag Target procedure is required by the WSB, the unloaded condition shall be governed by [Section F.2.2](#id-f.2.2-dmt-process-for-airsoft-gbb-systems) and subsequent stage procedures).*

### F.1.3 Eye & Ear Protection

#### F.1.3.1 Ear protection

Modifying [Section 1.6.2](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.6.2-ear-protection>): **Ear protection** is highly recommended, but not required to be worn by competitors or any individual on the range during shooting hours. Any associated safety penalties regarding ear protection are waived.

#### F.1.3.2 Loss mid-stage

Modifying [Section 1.6.3](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.6.3-loss-mid-stage>): The provisions of [Section 8.5](<08_Re-Shoots.md#id-8.5-loss-of-eye-ear-protection>) apply exclusively to the unintentional loss of **eye protection**.

### F.1.4 Minimum Target Engagement Distance for Steel Targets

Superseding [Section 1.8](<01_Range Safety & Procedures.md#id-1.8-minimum-target-engagement-distance-for-steel-targets>): Due to the significantly reduced kinetic energy of Airsoft projectiles, the minimum safe engagement distances for steel and rigid targets are modified as follows:
1. **Handgun:** No steel target shall be engaged within 3 meters.
2. **Rifle:** No steel target shall be engaged within 5 meters.

## F.2 Competitor Equipment Modifications

The following rules modify or supersede their respective sections in [Chapter 2](02_Competitor%20Equipment.md) for all affiliated Airsoft matches.

### F.2.1 Prohibited Firearms and Accessories

Sections [2.7.5](<02_Competitor Equipment.md#id-2.7.5-full-auto-or-burst-capable-firearms>), [2.7.5.1](<02_Competitor Equipment.md#id-2.7.5.1-match-director-exception>), and [2.7.6](<02_Competitor Equipment.md#id-2.7.6-simulated-full-auto-accessories>) do not apply. Firearms possessing full-automatic or burst capabilities, as well as simulated full-auto accessories, are permitted. However, competitors remain strictly subject to [Section 2.7.3](<02_Competitor Equipment.md#id-2.7.3-multiple-rounds-per-trigger-pull>) during the course of fire.

### F.2.2 DMT process for Airsoft GBB systems

Modifying [Section 2.9.2](<02_Competitor Equipment.md#id-2.9.2-dmt-process-for-firearms-with-a-magazine-disconnect>): When a formal DMT procedure is specified by the WSB, all Airsoft firearms are treated as firearms with a magazine disconnect. The competitor must utilize a designated empty magazine (containing propellant but no projectiles) to complete the following process:
1. Drop the loaded magazine and insert the empty magazine.
2. Engage the specified DMT with the single remaining chambered projectile.
3. Remove the empty magazine.
4. Pull the trigger with the firearm pointed in a safe direction to ensure the chamber is completely clear.
5. Resume standard procedures.

## F.4 Match Procedures Modifications

The following rules modify or supersede their respective sections in [Chapter 4](04_Match%20Procedures.md) for all affiliated Airsoft matches.

### F.4.1 Chronograph & Kinetic Energy Limits

Superseding [Section 4.5](<04_Match Procedures.md#id-4.5-chronograph-and-power-factor>): The Power Factor (PF) calculation system is not utilized in Airsoft matches. Instead, a strict maximum kinetic energy limit is enforced for all divisions.
1. **Maximum Energy Limit:** The absolute maximum kinetic energy allowed for any Airsoft firearm is 2.0 Joules.
2. **Local Law Precedence:** Competitors must strictly comply with all local and regional laws regarding Airsoft energy limits. If local legislation mandates a maximum kinetic energy limit lower than 2.0 Joules, the local legal limit shall strictly supersede the PCSL 2.0 Joules limit.
3. **Failing Chronograph:** Superseding [Section 4.5.3](<04_Match Procedures.md#id-4.5.3-failing-power-factor>): Any competitor whose firearm exceeds the applicable maximum kinetic energy limit during testing shall not be permitted to use that firearm in the match. Utilizing a firearm that exceeds the energy limits during a course of fire will result in a **Match DQ** for using prohibited equipment.

## F.7 Stage Procedures Modifications

The following rules modify or supersede their respective sections in [Chapter 7](07_Stage%20Procedures.md) for all affiliated Airsoft matches.

### F.7.1 Drop-Mag Target (DMT) Procedure

Modifying [Section 7.5](<07_Stage Procedures.md#id-7.5-drop-mag-target-dmt-procedure>): Due to Airsoft platforms utilizing propellant housed entirely within the magazine, Match Directors (MDs) may elect to implement one of the following two procedures for firearms transitioning to an unloaded state on the clock. The selected method must be explicitly defined in the Written Stage Brief (WSB).

#### F.7.1.1 Option 1: Magazine Removal Equivalency (Waiving DMT)

If specified by the WSB, the formal DMT engagement process is waived. A firearm is deemed an **Inert Firearm** immediately upon the complete removal of its magazine, regardless of whether a chambered projectile remains. The competitor may then abandon or transition the firearm according to the stage procedure.

#### F.7.1.2 Option 2: Airsoft DMT Process (Empty Magazine Requirement)

If the WSB requires a formally scored DMT procedure, it shall be executed by treating all Airsoft firearms as firearms with a magazine disconnect. The competitor must utilize a designated empty magazine (containing propellant but no projectiles) to complete the following process:

1. Drop the loaded magazine and insert the empty magazine.

2. Engage the specified DMT with the single remaining chambered projectile.

3. Remove the empty magazine.

4. Pull the trigger with the firearm pointed in a safe direction to ensure the chamber is completely clear.

5. Resume standard procedures.
